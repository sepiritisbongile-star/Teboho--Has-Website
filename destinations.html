Waterfall

Maletsunyane Falls

A single drop of about 190 m near Semonkong. The walk down takes about an hour; the climb back takes a little longer.

Mountain pass

Sani Pass

A steep 4x4 road to the border post at 2,874 m, with hairpins and loose rock near the top.

History

Thaba Bosiu

The flat-topped mountain where Moshoeshoe I held out in the 19th century, about 20 km from Maseru.

Dam

Katse Dam

A 185 m concrete wall deep in the highlands, holding back a long reservoir that runs between the mountains.

National park

Sehlabathebe

Remote grassland, rock pools and sandstone arches in the south-east, close to the South African border.

Culture

Morija

One of the oldest mission stations in the country, with a museum, an archive and a small printing press.

City

Maseru

The capital and the only real city. A useful base for day trips, with markets and government buildings.

Activity

Pony trekking

Guided rides from a few hours to several days. The Basotho pony is small, sure-footed and used to the paths.

A note on distances: allow more time than you think. A 100 km trip on gravel can take three hours, and mountain weather can slow things down further.

Practicalities

Where to stay

These are types of accommodation, not live listings. Confirm prices and availability directly with the place you choose.

Most towns have small guesthouses. Breakfast is not always included, and prices are often quoted per room. Ask about backup power before you book, because outages happen.

Maseru has the widest choice. Outside town, lodges cluster near places like Semonkong and Katse. Book ahead in December and around public holidays, when South African visitors tend to travel.

Camping is possible in some parks and along hiking routes. Check whether you need a permit, and do not assume there will be water or a shop nearby. Nights are cold, even in summer.
