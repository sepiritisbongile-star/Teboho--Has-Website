Lesotho · Southern Africa

Small country. Very big mountains.

Lesotho sits entirely above 1,000 m, and most of it is higher than 1,800. Come for the passes, the quiet villages and the night skies.

See destinations Plan a visit

Why Lesotho

A country you can cross on horseback

It is small, but the roads are slow, so a short distance on the map can take most of a day. That is part of the appeal. From Maseru you can be on a mountain pass within an hour, and in the highlands you will still meet people getting around by pony.

Lesotho is completely surrounded by South Africa, which surprises some first-time visitors. It has its own currency, two official languages and a winter ski slope that depends entirely on the weather.

Waterfall

Maletsunyane Falls

One drop of roughly 190 m near Semonkong, reached on foot or by pony. The spray is visible long before the bottom.

Details
Mountain pass

Sani Pass

The road climbs to the border post at 2,874 m. The last stretch has tight hairpins, loose rock and very little guard rail.

History

Thaba Bosiu

Moshoeshoe I's mountain stronghold, about 20 km from Maseru. The name means "mountain at night".

On the ground

Three ways to spend a day

01

Walk

Day hikes are easy to arrange around Semonkong and the Maloti. Take a guide if you are unsure about the route or the weather.

02

Ride

Basotho ponies handle steep paths better than most vehicles. Most guesthouses can put you in touch with a local guide.

03

Eat

Try papa (maize porridge) with moroho (greens). If you are curious, ask about joala, the home-brewed beer sold in rural areas.

Winter is the quiet season

June and July are cold. Snow can close the high passes without much warning, and that is when the mountains look their best. Pack a proper jacket.
